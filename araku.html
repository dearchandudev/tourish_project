The Valley

A journey beyond the ordinary.

Araku Valley is a scenic hill destination in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh. Green mountains, coffee plantations, waterfalls, caves and tribal heritage come together to create a destination that feels completely different from city life.

The journey to Araku is itself an experience — especially the famous mountain railway route, with tunnels, bridges, forests and dramatic valley views.

People & Heritage

A culture rooted in the forest.

Araku is home to several tribal communities whose lives and traditions have developed closely with the surrounding forests and mountains.

Their traditional knowledge, crafts, agriculture, music, dance, food and community life form an important part of the cultural identity of the valley.

01

Traditional Lifestyle

Community-oriented living closely connected with nature and agriculture.

02

Traditional Arts

Handicrafts, textiles, ornaments and locally inspired artwork.

03

Dance & Music

Group performances, traditional music and community celebrations.

04

Nature Knowledge

Generations of knowledge connected to forests, farming and local ecology.

Taste the mountains.

A visit to Araku isn't complete without exploring its coffee culture — from plantations to roasting and, of course, enjoying a fresh cup.

The Journey

The road to Araku is part of the story.

Travelling toward Araku through the Eastern Ghats can be as memorable as the destination itself. The railway route is famous for its mountain landscapes, tunnels, bridges and changing scenery.

Sit beside the window, slow down and watch the landscape transform from city surroundings into forests and mountains.

Travel Smart

Before you go.

🚆

By Train

The rail route through the Eastern Ghats is one of the most memorable ways to approach the valley.

🚗

By Road

Road travel offers beautiful mountain views. Check current road conditions before travelling.

🌤️

Weather

The valley is generally cooler than the coastal plains. Carry comfortable clothing and rain protection when appropriate.

🎒

What to Carry

Comfortable shoes, water, sunscreen, light clothing and a small backpack are useful for sightseeing.

📷

Photography

Keep your camera ready for mountains, waterfalls, forests, villages and misty landscapes.

🤝

Respect Local Culture

Ask before photographing people, respect local customs and avoid disturbing sacred or community spaces.
